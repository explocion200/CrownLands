# Estate artwork sources

The October 6 estate revisions use the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool and the user's uploaded atlas illustration, retained as `reference-style.png`. Thin brown outlines, matte painted shading, olive grass, ochre timber, warm pale stone and burgundy details guide the artwork. Character animation is deferred.

The selected integrated-ground and contextual Gatehouse revision uses these inputs:

- `terrain-integrated-v4.png`: selected 1448 × 1086 overview. Modest ground wear, paving and work yards are painted directly beneath the actual building bases and entrances. Terrain, worn roads, weathered walls, decorative cottages and gardens stay together. Runtime `terrain.webp` uses WebP quality 85 and normal regional-map dimensions. There is no standalone soil sprite.
- `building-bases-guide-v4.png`: development composite of the actual freestanding sprites in their fixed positions, used to place the ground beneath their footings. This guide is not runtime art.
- `terrain-detail-{nw,ne,sw,se}-v4.png`: four native close-up paintings generated from crops of the selected overview, including its integrated ground. Each covers a 748 × 567 logical region, with 48px overlap and roughly 1.9× source density. Runtime detail tiles retain those source pixels at WebP quality 85; visible tiles load only when camera density requires them. They add painted detail without enlarging the map's logical extent.
- `buildings-cohesive-v2.png`: unchanged transparent building atlas. Reviewed extraction bounds preserve whole towers, sails and service yards. Each atlas pixel represents .30 logical-map pixels; exports now retain native pixels at WebP quality 95, providing at least 3.2× density. Natural logical dimensions remain recorded in `sprite-sizes.json`.
- `gate-context-guide-v4.png` and `gate-context-painted-v4.png`: the actual southern wall crop, enlarged as a guide, then the Gatehouse painted in place. Two square guard towers, slate hip roofs, burgundy flags and warm worn stone match the enclosure. The guide uses the first ground study, whose wall geometry is retained in the selected terrain.
- `gate-extraction-mask-v4.svg`, `gatehouse-extracted-v4.png` and `gate-placement-v4.json`: reviewed alpha silhouette, original contextual masonry pixels and exact extraction coordinates. The arch is transparent. Native 677 × 279 pixels render at approximately 140.26 × 57.80 logical pixels, offset 5.70px east and 27.18px south inside the 10.5% × 10.5% reservation at the unchanged 50 / 60 center. The renderer recovers the cutout's painted location instead of stretching it to meet the walls.
- `plots-cohesive-v2.png`: unchanged painted surveyed and construction sources. Their runtime derivatives now retain more detail at 600 × 360, WebP quality 95, and fit the fixed reservation in either state.
- `layout-guide.svg` / `layout-guide.png`: registry-derived development placement guide, updated for the Gatehouse reservation. These guides are not runtime art.

[build-estate-art.js](../../../tools/build-estate-art.js) performs extraction, transparent trimming, resizing, WebP encoding and provenance recording with Sharp. [extract-estate-gate.js](../../../tools/extract-estate-gate.js) cuts the original in-map painting under the reviewed alpha mask and records its placement; it does not redraw the building. These utilities do not paint artwork. Use the bundled Node dependency path for Sharp; no client dependency was added. [create-estate-layout-guide.js](../../../tools/create-estate-layout-guide.js) regenerates the reference SVG. [create-estate-road-guide.js](../../../tools/create-estate-road-guide.js) uses `road-approaches-v3.json` to produce development-only approach repair guides in the ignored review directory.

[integrated-v4-prompts.json](./integrated-v4-prompts.json) retains the selected ground, contextual Gatehouse and close-up prompts, with the unselected ground/background-removal studies identified. [alignment-v3-prompts.json](./alignment-v3-prompts.json) and [cohesive-v2-prompts.json](./cohesive-v2-prompts.json) retain the earlier art prompts. `art-record.json` records hashes and dimensions for source inputs and all 27 runtime images; text hashes use canonical UTF-8/LF. Runtime assets are under `assets/inner-city-estate/`, outside installation precaching. The overview and site art budget is 1600 KiB; all estate art, including lazy close-ups, is bounded at 4096 KiB. Sources, references, prompts, utilities and review controls stay out of the production client.

The original sources, v2/v3 terrain and Gatehouse treatments, separate soil source and `terrain-yard-study-v4.png` remain archived. Their old actor atlas, SVG placeholders, geometric walls/roads, independent oval soil and rotating sails are unused. The current renderer has no ambient actors or animation loop.
