The Guild of Crown Lands

Approved balance for permanent members, prepared expeditions and a growing Guild interior. Groups 1–3 are approved for implementation; the full expansion is not deployed. The owner confirmed that individual starting stats must be random while favoring each type's specialties. This file is not imported by the game or server. Open the Guild in the local game preview.

Decisions that change the current specification: replace the Guild-level training ceiling with rarity ceilings; replace materials-only expeditions with separate reward categories; move future recovery improvements from the Alehouse to the Infirmary; add a five-minute introduction and one-hour short contracts alongside longer missions. Existing accepted missions retain their terms.

1. Members, training and recovery

Rarity ceilings: Common 20, Uncommon 40, Rare 60, Epic 80, Legendary 100. Rarity raises potential, without a second multiplier on starting stats. Keep the existing XP curve: advancing from level L costs 4 × L XP. Standard missions award each member 20 × nominal hours × difficulty multiplier XP; difficulty multipliers remain 1 / 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.3 / 1.4.

Rarity replaces the Guild-level champion ceiling. Guild levels continue to unlock better contracts, larger parties, simultaneous missions and interior improvements. At a rarity ceiling, existing banked XP is retained; new XP stops. No promotion, fusion, deletion, sale or champion equipment is introduced.

Random starting stats — direction confirmed: every recruit has individual rolls, with higher ranges in their type's specialties. Approved implementation: independently roll each stat uniformly from its listed center minus 2 through center plus 2. For example, a Guard rolls Strength 10–14, Agility 4–8, Knowledge 4–8, Leadership 6–10 and Endurance 8–12. The following table gives approved centers, not fixed starting stats. The owner approved these exact ranges.

The server creates and saves rolls when the recruitment offer is first generated, exposes them before purchase, and copies those exact values to the hired member. Refreshes, retries and season changes never reroll an existing offer or acquired member. Existing members receive stable new metadata through migration without changing their earned identity, level or XP.

Each member receives one stable mission trait: Roadwise (ambush), Pathfinder (wilderness), Meticulous (investigation), Tinkerer (engineering), Diplomatic (negotiation), or Prepared (supplies). A matching trait removes 2 percentage points of risk, capped at 6 points per party. Types, traits, portraits and names are stored, never rerolled on reload or season reset. A matching signature removes 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 percentage points at champion levels 1 / 10 / 20 / 40 / 60; combined signatures remove at most 12 points. Medic instead reduces recovery by 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 / 10%, using only the best Medic. Milestones require no extra purchase.

Recovery: base rest is 15 minutes per nominal mission hour, multiplied by 1 / 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.35 / 1.5 for difficulty. Partial success multiplies rest by 1.25; failure by 1.5. Infirmary, meals and Medic reductions add, with a combined 55% cap and 10-minute minimum. The introduction is the sole exception: two minutes of rest. Rest begins at the accepted completion deadline, not when a player opens a report or claims rewards. Everyone recovers concurrently.

Alehouse transition: capture each account's already-earned Alehouse reduction (up to 35%) as a permanent recovery entitlement. Use the higher of that entitlement or the Infirmary reduction, then apply meals and Medic. Do not stack Alehouse and Infirmary. Future Alehouse levels improve recruitment and meal access; already accepted meals and missions stay unchanged.

Legacy ceiling policy: keep every existing level and XP balance. An individual member's protected ceiling is the greater of their rarity ceiling, their current earned level, and a higher level already promised by an accepted legacy mission. Never remove banked XP. Old quests finish with their stored training results; only later launches use new rules.

Pacing example: Common level 1 → 20 costs 760 XP, or 38 successful standard mission hours before mentoring and personal milestones. Legendary level 1 → 100 costs 19,800 XP, or 990 standard mission hours. Failure XP helps progress, but does not grant reputation or main rewards.

Outcomes: full success pays 100% main rewards and XP; partial success pays 50% fungible rewards and 75% XP; failure pays no main reward and 50% XP. Secondary objectives and Gear require full success. Every successful mission grants one Guild reputation and one point with its named client. The introductory mission grants neither repeatable allowance nor reputation.

Gear probabilities: after full success, the independent 40% find roll yields Common 90% / Uncommon 10% when the chosen officer building has an earned Uncommon entitlement; otherwise Common 100%. Rare, Epic and Legendary cannot drop. Existing item families for that officer are equally likely. The player chooses the officer before launch and sees overall odds: success probability × 40% × rarity share ÷ eligible item families. No matching-copy requirements, prices, promotions, commissions or entitlements change. This is an explicit new acquisition exception, not a free rarity upgrade.

Introduction: once per account, five minutes, one hired member, one Food, no meal/supplies/route modifiers, no failure. Reward: 10 XP for the member and one story discovery; two-minute recovery. No free member or Gold is supplied. The guide points to the Alehouse and food chain if needed.

Season rule: Gold and troops are seasonal. New Gold/troop contracts cannot start if their accepted completion would cross the current realm's season boundary. Claims go only to their original, still-current realm/Main City; unclaimed seasonal Gold/troops expire at that season's reset, clearly disclosed in the briefing. Permanent XP, reputation, reports, materials and Gear persist. Gold/troop amounts lock to the unboosted Main City rate at launch. An account's daily reservations and Gear cooldown do not reset when switching realms or seasons.

Budget comparison: the Gold ceiling is 4.17% of one day's unboosted Main City output; the troop ceiling is 2.08%. A Gear launch produces at most 0.38 expected items every 72 hours at the 95% maximum success chance. Only low-rarity Gear drops, protecting the value of matching-copy progression and commissions. These approved limits must be validated with simulations; approval is not a claim of proven live balance.

Mentoring: one active mentor/pupil assignment at a time. Mentor must be ready, level 10 or higher, and at least five levels above the pupil. Ambitions: each member has a type-themed personal chain requiring 1, then 3, then 6 cumulative successful matching assignments. One-time milestone rewards are 20 / 60 / 120 XP and a recorded personal story/title. Those rewards obey the member's protected rarity ceiling and cannot be farmed by replaying reports.

Reputation and stories: Guild/client thresholds are 1 / 10 / 30 / 60 / 100 successful contracts. Five client contracts and five Archive chapters become available through corresponding room improvements and reputation. Chapter discoveries are permanent; they unlock follow-up contracts and narrative choices rather than passive world combat bonuses. Histories record departures, outcomes, milestone gains and memorable contributions. Account-wide completed-contract titles arrive at 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 successes; they grant no currency or combat stat.

Compatibility and release boundaries

  • Legacy members keep their IDs, names, ownership, rarity, levels, XP and recovery deadlines. Add stable type, portrait, trait and history metadata without replacement.
  • Accepted legacy quests settle through their existing versioned terms, including rewards, training snapshot, Food cost and recovery. Existing material parcels, commissions and all action receipts remain redeemable/replay-safe.
  • New missions snapshot their members, leader, choices, costs, risk, reward tables, realm and allowance reservation. Server-only entropy resolves an outcome exactly once; no client seed, report action or reload can alter it.
  • Full storage or Gear inventory leaves the unclaimed reward safely recorded. Partial claims only remove items actually received. Seasonal Gold/troop expiry is disclosed and separate from permanent inventory.
  • New clients and server rules use the existing release-contract checks; incompatible clients cannot silently send legacy quest actions under the new contract.
  • Existing recruitment fees, active/bench limits, officer upgrades, economy building progression, estate positions and map navigation stay unchanged unless this review explicitly says otherwise.
