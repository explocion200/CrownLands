The gold coin itself should have warm muted antique gold, ochre and brown-gold shading, a few restrained pale warm highlights, and subtle hand-painted metallic texture. A subtly irregular hand-hammered circular shape, a clearly defined simple raised rim with sparse engraved marks, small worn nicks and natural patina. In the center, a large bold embossed royal crown with three clear points, broad simple silhouette, subtly dark recessed outline and gently shaded raised gold relief; the crown should be the unmistakable focal symbol and occupy most of the face. Render the icon with authentic painted material character and careful edges, not glossy modern 3D, photoreal photography, flat vector art, plastic, neon, saturated bright yellow, or cartoon sparkle.
