Draft for approval

Four personal weeks, carried across seasons. Days 5–6 bring larger resource rewards and items; days 7, 14, 21 and 28 include a Common Gear Box. Select any day to inspect its complete bundle. Controls above simulate time and resets; they do not change a game account.

Interactive approval draft

The proposed cycle

28 login days · 111 hours of Gold · 111 hours of troops · six items · four Gear Boxes. Every cycle uses this same budget with a new arrangement. Early-week days give 2–6 production hours, days 5–6 give 8–13 hours, and each seventh day gives 16 or 20 hours plus a chest. These exact amounts and bundles are proposed for approval.

Missed days pause progress. Up to two earned rewards wait for collection and carry across seasons. Finishing Day 28 starts the next cycle without granting an extra daily attendance reward. Quests and Achievements will be handled separately.

Draft only: the live game still uses its existing monthly rules. Season reset here preserves the track and queued rewards, then requires a sample main city before claiming. Sample resource amounts use that city's new production.
