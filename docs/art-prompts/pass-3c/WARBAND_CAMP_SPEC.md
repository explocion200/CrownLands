# Warband Camp Production Specification

## Role

The Warband Camp is an active temporary military encampment where soldiers gather, drill, rest, and prepare to march.

## Scale Standard

- Source master: 640x640 PNG with transparent background.
- Optimized runtime canvas: 384x384 WebP with preserved transparent padding.
- Runtime map render size: 132px square.
- Safe area: primary camp mass should fit inside roughly 72-78% of the source canvas width and 58-68% of the source canvas height, with a compact ground footprint smaller than a Stronghold.

## Composition

Standalone Crownlands map-object camp, elevated strategy-game view, same pitch and viewing direction as the approved city stages and Strongholds. Use a compact dirt/straw base and visible front approach.

Include two or three military tents, spear and shield racks, a practice area, campfire, supply cart, small perimeter stakes, troop banners, woodpiles, and soldiers or instructors where scale permits. The physical camp communicates troops; do not use a giant sword symbol.

## Materials And Palette

Canvas, timber poles, rope, leather, iron weapon tips, painted wooden shields, packed dirt, straw, smoke, faded burgundy and blue banners, weathered gray metal. Use warm earth and timber with military oxblood and muted cloth accents.

## Activity

Tiny soldiers may drill, stand guard, or gather around racks. Keep activity subordinate to the silhouette.

## Transparency

Generate on a perfectly flat #ff00ff chroma-key background. No sky, rectangular terrain plate, gradient backdrop, detached shadow, text, labels, or UI symbols. A small irregular dirt/straw base directly under the camp is allowed.

## Negative Instructions

No fortress walls, no giant sword icon, no magical aura, no tournament arena, no fantasy spikes, no modern military insignia.
