# Deed Camp Production Specification

## Role

The Deed Camp is a Crownlands administrative objective where land, title, and authority are formally recorded and granted.

## Scale Standard

- Source master: 640x640 PNG with transparent background.
- Optimized runtime canvas: 384x384 WebP with preserved transparent padding.
- Runtime map render size: 132px square.
- Safe area: primary camp mass should fit inside roughly 72-78% of the source canvas width and 58-68% of the source canvas height, with a compact ground footprint smaller than a Stronghold.

## Composition

Standalone Crownlands map-object camp, elevated strategy-game view, same pitch and viewing direction as the approved city stages and Strongholds. Use a compact dirt/grass base with a herald's pavilion as the main silhouette.

Include a writing desk or map table, large parchment charter, wax seals, records chest, rolled maps, document cases, survey stakes, land markers, clerk/herald area, guards, and a Crownlands banner. The identity comes from administration, documents, seals, and survey tools.

## Materials And Palette

Canvas, timber poles, parchment, red wax, wooden chests, leather document cases, rope, brass clasps, packed earth, linen, burgundy and faded-blue heraldic cloth.

## Activity

Tiny clerk, herald, or guards may appear if scale permits. Preserve readability at runtime size.

## Transparency

Generate on a perfectly flat #ff00ff chroma-key background. No sky, rectangular terrain plate, gradient backdrop, detached shadow, text, labels, or UI symbols. A small irregular dirt/straw base directly under the camp is allowed.

## Negative Instructions

No magical parchment glow, no giant scroll icon, no modern paperwork symbols, no palace tent, no fantasy tribunal, no text baked into art.
