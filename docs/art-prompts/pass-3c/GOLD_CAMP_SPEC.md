# Gold Camp Production Specification

## Role

The Gold Camp is a temporary economic and revenue objective. It stores collected wealth, goods, records, and guarded merchant supplies without becoming a fortified Stronghold.

## Scale Standard

- Source master: 640x640 PNG with transparent background.
- Optimized runtime canvas: 384x384 WebP with preserved transparent padding.
- Runtime map render size: 132px square.
- Safe area: primary camp mass should fit inside roughly 72-78% of the source canvas width and 58-68% of the source canvas height, with a compact ground footprint smaller than a Stronghold.

## Composition

Standalone Crownlands map-object camp, elevated strategy-game view, same pitch and viewing direction as the approved city stages and Strongholds. Use a compact dirt and straw footprint with a road approach from the lower/front side.

Include merchant wagons, a guarded tax chest, crates, barrels, sacks, a counting table, a small scale, a temporary awning, modest perimeter stakes or rope, and one restrained Crownlands banner. Gold appears only as small coins, brass scale parts, locked chests, or sealed treasury crates.

## Materials And Palette

Canvas, rough timber, rope, leather, packed earth, straw, crates, muted brass, burgundy/faded-blue/ochre cloth accents, parchment record tags. Use warm earth, ochre, timber brown, parchment, moss, weathered gray, and restrained burgundy.

## Activity

Tiny guards, merchants, wagon handlers, or clerks may appear if scale permits. Keep the silhouette clear at gameplay size.

## Transparency

Generate on a perfectly flat #ff00ff chroma-key background. No sky, rectangular terrain plate, gradient backdrop, detached shadow, text, labels, or UI symbols. A small irregular dirt/straw base directly under the camp is allowed.

## Negative Instructions

No giant coin icon, no gold pile shrine, no gold-colored camp, no magical glow, no banking imagery, no modern tents, no fantasy treasure altar.
