# Relic Camp Production Specification

## Role

The Relic Camp is a guarded temporary shrine or pavilion where a valuable object is protected and revered.

## Scale Standard

- Source master: 640x640 PNG with transparent background.
- Optimized runtime canvas: 384x384 WebP with preserved transparent padding.
- Runtime map render size: 132px square.
- Safe area: primary camp mass should fit inside roughly 72-78% of the source canvas width and 58-68% of the source canvas height, with a compact ground footprint smaller than a Stronghold.

## Composition

Standalone Crownlands map-object camp, elevated strategy-game view, same pitch and viewing direction as the approved city stages and Strongholds. Use a compact dirt/grass/straw base with a guarded central pavilion.

Include a small reliquary chest or shrine, canvas canopy, candles, pale ceremonial cloth, carved wooden or stone marker, guards, simple fencing or ropes, and modest supply crates. Importance should come from protection, ceremony, and care rather than magic.

## Materials And Palette

Pale canvas, parchment cloth, rough timber, wax candles, fieldstone marker, muted brass, restrained gilt on a small reliquary, burgundy and linen cloth, packed earth, straw, soot. Use warm candle points but keep overall daylight consistent.

## Activity

Tiny guards or attendants may appear if scale permits. Keep the protected central object readable but not oversized.

## Transparency

Generate on a perfectly flat #ff00ff chroma-key background. No sky, rectangular terrain plate, gradient backdrop, detached shadow, text, labels, or UI symbols. A small irregular dirt/straw base directly under the camp is allowed.

## Negative Instructions

No blue magic, no purple glow, no glowing crystal, no floating relic, no magical beam, no fantasy altar effects, no giant item icon.
