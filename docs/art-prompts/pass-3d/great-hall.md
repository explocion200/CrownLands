# Great Hall

- Exact target dimensions: 1254x1254 opaque PNG source, 512x512 optimized WebP runtime.
- Runtime display dimensions: square preview image in the Inner Castle tray.
- Aspect ratio: 1:1.
- Camera: consistent three-quarter interior hall view, human scale.
- Composition: raised dais, modest ruler chair, long tables, hearth, tapestries, narrow windows, carved timber roof.
- Lighting: daylight through windows mixed with restrained hearth/candle light.
- Materials: warm gray stone, aged oak, wool banners, linen, iron, candles, worn floor.
- Humans/props: steward, retainers, servants or envoys; people support the room but do not dominate.
- UI safe zones: dais and tables remain readable when scaled down.
- Heraldry: burgundy/faded-blue Crownlands banners and painted shields.
- Negative instructions: no giant fantasy throne, glowing chandeliers, cathedral scale, pristine marble, magical stained glass, or text.
- Function: communicate ruler authority, judgments, feasts, and formal kingdom discussion.
- Officer visual inheritance: future royal or steward characters should inherit sober ceremonial cloth, carved wood, banners, and restrained authority.
