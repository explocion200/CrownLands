# Royal Stables

- Exact target dimensions: 1254x1254 opaque PNG source, 512x512 optimized WebP runtime.
- Runtime display dimensions: square preview image in the Inner Castle tray.
- Aspect ratio: 1:1.
- Camera: consistent three-quarter stable/courtyard view.
- Composition: stalls, horses, tack, saddles, bridles, hay, water trough, courier bags, road/courtyard access.
- Lighting: natural stable daylight with restrained interior shadow.
- Materials: timber, stone floor, straw, leather, rope, iron fittings, wool blankets, mud.
- Humans/props: stable hands, riders or couriers, feed, saddle racks, buckets.
- UI safe zones: horses and tack must remain readable at 512px and mobile preview sizes.
- Heraldry: subtle Crownlands horse blankets or cloth marks.
- Negative instructions: no magical horses, oversized fantasy mounts, glowing tack, racing modernity, wings, lightning, or text.
- Function: communicate cavalry, courier movement, and logistical readiness.
- Officer visual inheritance: Cavalry Master should inherit leather tack, riding boots, gloves, courier seals, horse blankets, road dust, and practical cavalry gear.
