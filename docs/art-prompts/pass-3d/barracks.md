# Barracks

- Exact target dimensions: 1254x1254 opaque PNG source, 512x512 optimized WebP runtime.
- Runtime display dimensions: square preview image in the Inner Castle tray.
- Aspect ratio: 1:1.
- Camera: consistent three-quarter military room with yard connection if possible.
- Composition: racks, spears, shields, armor bench, benches or bunks, visible training/muster cues.
- Lighting: natural daylight from yard/doors with restrained indoor shadow.
- Materials: stone, oak, iron, leather, wool, mail, gambeson cloth, rough benches.
- Humans/props: soldiers, armorer, equipment crates, shields, helmets, campaign banners.
- UI safe zones: weapon racks and soldiers should remain recognizable at 512px and mobile preview sizes.
- Heraldry: Crownlands military banners, faded cloth, no separate faction language.
- Negative instructions: no oversized fantasy weapons, glowing armory, modern uniforms, firearms, spikes, skull motifs, or text.
- Function: communicate troop production, training, and military organization.
- Officer visual inheritance: War Captain should inherit practical mail/brigandine, campaign banners, disciplined equipment, and tool-worn military surroundings.
