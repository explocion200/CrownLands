# Alehouse

- Exact target dimensions: 1254x1254 opaque PNG source, 512x512 optimized WebP runtime.
- Runtime display dimensions: square preview image in the Inner Castle tray.
- Aspect ratio: 1:1.
- Camera: consistent three-quarter room view, warm but not theatrical.
- Composition: benches, trestle tables, barrels, mugs, hearth, serving counter, simple food, worn floor.
- Lighting: daylight and hearth/candlelight with realistic exposure; warm and alive, not orange fantasy glow.
- Materials: timber, plaster, stone hearth, barrels, linen, clay mugs, iron hooks, herbs, soot.
- Humans/props: soldiers, workers, travelers, server; small social activity.
- UI safe zones: tables, hearth, and barrels should remain readable at preview size.
- Heraldry: modest Crownlands cloth accents, not tavern-brand signage.
- Negative instructions: no modern pub layout, neon tavern, fantasy sign, luxury inn, glowing drinks, or text.
- Function: communicate morale, rest, recovery, and everyday social life inside a working fortress.
