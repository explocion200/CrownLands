# PWA / Installed App Identity

- Function: favicon, launcher icon, installed-app identity, and Crownlands brand anchor.
- Master: 1254x1254 opaque PNG. Derive 192x192 and 512x512 normal plus maskable variants; derive 32x32 favicon.
- Composition: unified simple hammered Crownlands crown above a compact three-merlon stone gate silhouette, centered on a weathered burgundy heraldic field. A thin irregular parchment/ochre keyline may separate the emblem from the field.
- Safe zone: entire crown-and-gate emblem inside the central 56% for maskable cropping; essential crown silhouette inside 48%.
- Materials/light: flat heraldic read with subtly physical cloth field, muted gilt crown, warm gray stone/iron gate; restrained upper-left modeling.
- Readability: crown remains dominant at 16-32px; gate becomes supporting base at larger sizes.
- Negative: no text, swords, army, map, scroll, multiple banners, circular neon border, glossy app tile, giant gemstone, blue field, or photographic scene.
