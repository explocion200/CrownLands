# Map

- Function: regional and world map navigation.
- Canvas: 1254x1254 RGBA; runtime 192x192; subject fills 76%.
- Composition: folded parchment campaign map opened in a broad zigzag, ink road, river bend, tiny fort marks, and a restrained eight-direction ink rose drawn directly on parchment.
- Materials/light: aged parchment, charcoal and faded-indigo ink, leather tie; soft upper-left daylight.
- Readability: bold folded silhouette and clearly drawn routes.
- Negative: no GPS pin, globe, telescope, shiny compass device, readable words, or magical terrain.
