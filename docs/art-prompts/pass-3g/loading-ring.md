# Loading Ring

- Function: slowly rotating outer Crownlands loading medallion.
- Canvas: 1254x1254 RGBA; runtime 256x256; ring fills 92% while center stays transparent.
- Composition: circular stamped dull-iron ring with restrained manuscript hatch marks, four small muted-brass binding studs, shallow engraved linework, and slight hand-forged irregularity. No directional top ornament that becomes visually distracting during rotation.
- Materials/light: hammered iron, minimal tarnished brass; near-front, soft upper-left daylight.
- Readability: one bold circular ring with clean hollow center.
- Negative: no letters, runes, blue enamel, floral gilding, gems, magic, glow, clock markings, or white center.
