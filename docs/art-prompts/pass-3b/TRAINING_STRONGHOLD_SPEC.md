# Training Stronghold Production Specification

## Role

The Training Stronghold is a fortified military mustering and training center. It represents troop production, discipline, barracks, drills, and armory organization.

## Reference

Use the approved Pass 3A Stage 5 city and Crown Citadel as the primary architectural references. This asset must belong to the same Crownlands civilization.

## Composition

- Standalone map objective token with clean transparent final background.
- Elevated strategy-game view, matching Pass 3A camera pitch and viewing direction.
- Practical fortified barracks compound with a central drill yard.
- Barracks, armory, shield racks, weapon racks, archery targets, practice dummies, supply sheds, muster banners, and a guarded gate.
- Strong enough to be a major objective, but visually below Crown Citadel.

## Materials

Fieldstone walls, timber barracks, limewashed plaster, oak beams, wood shingles, packed earth training yard, iron hardware, leather equipment, canvas/cloth drill awnings, burgundy and faded-blue banners.

## Palette

Weathered gray, timber brown, packed earth, charcoal, faded blue, burgundy, muted brass, parchment cloth.

## Activity

Tiny soldiers drilling, instructors, guards, targets, and equipment stacks where scale permits. Avoid crowding the image.

## Transparency

Generate on a flat #ff00ff chroma-key background for local alpha removal. No sky, no rectangular terrain plate, no gradient background, no detached cast shadow. A compact packed-earth yard edge is allowed.

## Negative Instructions

No text, no labels, no giant crossed-swords symbol, no weapon statues, no glowing barracks, no esports colors, no magical military aura.

## Intended Gameplay Size

Source master may be larger for quality, but optimized runtime target is a transparent objective WebP around 384px max edge.
