# Gold Stronghold Production Specification

## Role

The Gold Stronghold is a fortified merchant and treasury complex. It represents trade, taxation, counting, guarded storage, and economic infrastructure.

## Reference

Use the approved Pass 3A Stage 5 city and Crown Citadel as the primary architectural references. This asset must look built by the same Crownlands masons, carpenters, roofers, and banner makers.

## Composition

- Standalone map objective token with clean transparent final background.
- Elevated strategy-game view, matching Pass 3A camera pitch and viewing direction.
- Compact fortified compound, smaller and less royal than Crown Citadel but more specialized than a normal city.
- Guarded road approach entering from the lower/front side.
- Fortified counting house or treasury at the center.
- Storehouses, guarded warehouse doors, wagons, barrels, crates, weighing area, and a busy trade courtyard.
- Small coin chests may appear sparingly, but the building must not be golden.

## Materials

Fieldstone walls, dressed stone treasury, oak gates, timber-framed storage, clay tile on richer buildings, wood shingles, packed dirt, iron hardware, rope, parchment-colored cloth awnings, muted brass fittings, burgundy Crownlands banners.

## Palette

Warm earth, ochre, timber brown, weathered gray, parchment cloth, muted brass, restrained burgundy. Avoid bright gold; brass accents only.

## Activity

Tiny merchants, guards, wagons, crates, and counting-yard details where scale permits. Keep the silhouette readable.

## Transparency

Generate on a flat #ff00ff chroma-key background for local alpha removal. No sky, no rectangular terrain plate, no gradient background, no detached cast shadow. A compact dirt/road/stone base immediately under the compound is allowed.

## Negative Instructions

No text, no labels, no bonus percentages, no giant coin icon, no gold-plated castle, no magical glow, no neon, no modern bank imagery, no fantasy treasure mountain.

## Intended Gameplay Size

Source master may be larger for quality, but optimized runtime target is a transparent objective WebP around 384px max edge.
