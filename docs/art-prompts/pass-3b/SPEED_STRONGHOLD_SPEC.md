# Speed Stronghold Production Specification

## Role

The Speed Stronghold is a fortified cavalry logistics and courier relay station. It represents movement, road control, rapid deployment, horses, riders, and messenger infrastructure.

## Reference

Use the approved Pass 3A Stage 5 city and Crown Citadel as the primary architectural references. This asset must share their stone, timber, roof, daylight, and road language.

## Composition

- Standalone map objective token with clean transparent final background.
- Elevated strategy-game view, matching Pass 3A camera pitch and viewing direction.
- Compact defensive walls around stables, horse yards, courier lodge, messenger pavilion, tack storage, hay/feed stores, hitching posts, and open gate.
- Broad road connection should be a major visible feature entering from the lower/front side and passing through or beside the gate.
- Saddled horses, riders, handlers, and courier carts where scale permits.

## Materials

Fieldstone walls, timber stable structures, oak gates, thatch or wood shingles, packed road dirt, straw, hay, rope, leather tack, iron fittings, faded-blue and burgundy banners.

## Palette

Warm dirt, timber brown, moss, faded blue, weathered gray, straw ochre, muted brass, restrained burgundy.

## Activity

Tiny riders, horse handlers, saddled horses, and wagon/courier details. Function should read physically, not symbolically.

## Transparency

Generate on a flat #ff00ff chroma-key background for local alpha removal. No sky, no rectangular terrain plate, no gradient background, no detached cast shadow. A compact road and yard base is allowed.

## Negative Instructions

No text, no labels, no lightning, no glowing trails, no winged horses, no magical speed symbols, no modern road signs, no futuristic effects.

## Intended Gameplay Size

Source master may be larger for quality, but optimized runtime target is a transparent objective WebP around 384px max edge.
