# Defense Stronghold Production Specification

## Role

The Defense Stronghold is the most obviously defensive of the four role Strongholds. It represents walls, controlled entry, layered fortification, and defensive readiness.

## Reference

Use the approved Pass 3A Stage 5 city and Crown Citadel as the primary architectural references. This asset should feel like a specialist military fortress built by the same Crownlands culture, but still below Crown Citadel in hierarchy.

## Composition

- Standalone map objective token with clean transparent final background.
- Elevated strategy-game view, matching Pass 3A camera pitch and viewing direction.
- Thick curtain walls, substantial towers, reinforced gatehouse, layered inner yard, battlements, ditch or earthwork, narrow controlled entrance, protected supply structures.
- The silhouette should say difficult to take through real fortification, not a giant shield motif.

## Materials

Heavy fieldstone and dressed stone, oak gate, iron portcullis, timber hoarding where appropriate, packed earth, stone steps, defensive stores, rope, leather, faded cloth banners.

## Palette

Weathered gray stone, charcoal, timber brown, packed earth, rust, oxblood/burgundy accents, faded blue used sparingly, muted brass only for hardware.

## Activity

Tiny guards, sentries, stored defensive supplies, spear racks, and readiness details where scale permits.

## Transparency

Generate on a flat #ff00ff chroma-key background for local alpha removal. No sky, no rectangular terrain plate, no gradient background, no detached cast shadow. A compact earthwork/ditch edge is allowed.

## Negative Instructions

No text, no labels, no giant shield-shaped castle, no force field, no glowing wall, no impossible fantasy fortress, no polished palace styling.

## Intended Gameplay Size

Source master may be larger for quality, but optimized runtime target is a transparent objective WebP around 384px max edge.
