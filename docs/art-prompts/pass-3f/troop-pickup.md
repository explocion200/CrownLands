# Troop Pickup

Create a compact grounded muster bundle: one dented practical kettle helmet in front of a short burgundy military banner, a rolled recruitment order with oxblood wax seal, and two short spear shafts crossing behind. Keep the parts tightly grouped as one small readable token for soldiers. Follow the Pass 3F shared specification. Avoid floating soldiers, giant crossed weapons, blue-and-gold fantasy heraldry, weapon trophies, glow, and modern insignia.
