# Recall Horn

Create a rugged late-medieval military signaling horn made from natural cream-and-brown animal horn with dull brass mouthpiece and rim, stitched leather carrying strap, burgundy wool cord, and a small engraved Crownlands crown-and-wall mark. Strong curved silhouette, practical campaign wear. Follow the Pass 3F shared specification. Avoid blue enamel, gold plating, magical glow, drinking-horn form, and modern trumpet valves.
