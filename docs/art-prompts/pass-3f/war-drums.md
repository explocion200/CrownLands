# War Drums

Create one compact late-medieval campaign drum with hide head, oak body, leather tension cords, two crossed wooden drumsticks, worn burgundy-and-ochre painted Crownlands bands, and modest campaign wear. The real drum should communicate mustering and troop production at small scale. Follow the Pass 3F shared specification. Avoid a modern drum kit, paired fantasy drums, blue lacquer, gold plating, runes, sound waves, and glow.
