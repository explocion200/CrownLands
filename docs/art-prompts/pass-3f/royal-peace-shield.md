# Royal Peace Shield

Create a grounded Crownlands symbol of temporary royal protection: a practical late-medieval wooden heater shield with dull iron rim and burgundy painted field, crossed by a folded safe-conduct proclamation fixed with a deep oxblood royal wax seal and small restrained brass crown fitting. The shield and sealed charter must remain one compact silhouette. Follow the Pass 3F shared specification. Avoid energy bubbles, blue enamel, glowing edges, magic, giant crowns, and ornate tournament fantasy.
