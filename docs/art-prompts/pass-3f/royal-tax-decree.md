# Royal Tax Decree

Create a folded royal financial order as the primary object: irregular warm parchment with sparse illegible ink marks, deep oxblood Crownlands wax seal, linen tie, a small tally stick, muted brass balance weight, and three irregular worn hammered coins. Keep the document dominant and the coins restrained. Follow the Pass 3F shared specification. Avoid readable writing, scroll finials, coin piles, glowing gold, pristine paper, and fantasy royal ornament.
