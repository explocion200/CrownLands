# Gold Pickup

Create a small worn brown leather coin purse with loosened drawstring and six to eight irregular hammered medieval coins spilling partly from it. Add a tiny tax seal tag and restrained warm metal highlights. The purse remains the main mass and the silhouette must read immediately as movable gold at map scale. Follow the Pass 3F shared specification. Avoid perfect coin circles, giant coin pile, open treasure bag, bright yellow saturation, sparkles, and glow.
