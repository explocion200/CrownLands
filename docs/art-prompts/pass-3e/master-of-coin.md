# Master of Coin Production Specification

## Portrait

- Function: prosperous, educated male royal administrator responsible for taxation, ledgers, and Crownlands finances.
- Reference environment: `assets/inner-castle/treasury.png`.
- Identity: Crownlands man in his late 50s, broad thoughtful face, receding gray hair, neatly trimmed gray beard, observant expression and controlled posture.
- Clothing: quality burgundy and charcoal wool robe with linen layers, modest fur collar, dark leather belt, restrained brass chain and keys.
- Equipment: substantial bound royal ledger with wax seals, a quill case, and treasury keys; no conventional weapon.
- Composition: full-body three-quarter view in the secure Treasury, counting table, scales, chests, parchment, and clerk activity secondary in depth.
- Lighting: natural window light with restrained candle warmth; wealth conveyed through materials and control, not piles of gold.

## Common Gear Set

- Head: soft burgundy wool chaperon/cap with restrained fur edge.
- Chest: quality charcoal-burgundy administrative robe with linen collar and modest fur trim.
- Pants: tailored dark wool hose/breeches appropriate beneath the robe.
- Boots: well-made dark leather court shoes/ankle boots with sealed straps.
- Gloves: thin ledger gloves with an ink-stained fingertip and folded linen cuff.
- Belt: administrative belt with keys, seal matrix, document case, and small tally pouch.
- Weapon/tool: closed oak-and-leather royal ledger with parchment tabs, wax seals, and a compact brass counting scale attached. Absolutely no blade, mace, staff weapon, or martial silhouette.
- Necklace: restrained treasury chain with a small royal seal and key token.

## Officer Visual Inheritance

The portrait and gear inherit the Treasury's dressed stone, dark oak desks, parchment, wax, locks, scales, quiet administration, and restrained material wealth.
