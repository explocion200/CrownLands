# War Captain Production Specification

## Portrait

- Function: seasoned male field commander responsible for troop production and attack strength.
- Reference environment: `assets/inner-castle/barracks.png`.
- Identity: Crownlands man in his early-to-mid 40s, weathered angular face, short dark hair with gray at the temples, close beard, calm command expression.
- Clothing: burgundy wool surcoat over a practical quilted gambeson, mail at neck and arms, restrained brigandine/plate reinforcement, campaign belt, worn gloves and boots.
- Equipment: sheathed officer sword and a rolled muster order; no heroic posing.
- Composition: full-body three-quarter view in the Barracks, weapon racks and working soldiers secondary in depth, clear negative space around silhouette for UI slots.
- Lighting: soft courtyard daylight entering the darker Barracks, restrained warm practical light.

## Common Gear Set

- Head: practical iron sallet/kettle-style command helm with burgundy cloth tie.
- Chest: burgundy brigandine over a quilted gambeson with dull iron rivets.
- Pants: dark wool joined hose with mail/iron knee reinforcement.
- Boots: worn calf-high campaign boots with mud-darkened soles.
- Gloves: leather officer gauntlets with restrained iron backs.
- Belt: broad campaign belt with scabbard loops, map case, and small utility pouch.
- Weapon: human-scale late-medieval officer sword in a worn leather scabbard.
- Necklace: small heraldic command badge on a cord, shaped as a Crownlands seal rather than a modern medal.

## Officer Visual Inheritance

The portrait and gear inherit the Barracks' dark oak racks, practical iron equipment, burgundy muster cloth, stone floor, and visibly maintained campaign wear.
