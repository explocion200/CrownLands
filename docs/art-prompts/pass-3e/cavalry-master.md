# Cavalry Master Production Specification

## Portrait

- Function: experienced male cavalry, courier, reinforcement, and horse-logistics officer.
- Reference environment: `assets/inner-castle/royal-stables.png`.
- Identity: lean Crownlands man in his late 30s, sun-weathered face, long nose, sandy hair tied at the nape, short moustache and beard, alert practical expression.
- Clothing: moss/faded-blue riding coat with burgundy heraldic tab, reinforced leather, light mail at shoulders, riding gloves, tall boots, spurs, courier belt.
- Equipment: reins in one hand, compact cavalry sword sheathed, dispatch case; horses and stable hands remain background context.
- Composition: full-body three-quarter view in the Royal Stables with saddle/tack wall and open courtyard light, clear UI-safe silhouette.
- Lighting: soft natural stable daylight with believable timber shadow.

## Common Gear Set

- Head: practical light cavalry sallet/riding helm with leather liner and small faded cloth tie.
- Chest: fitted leather-and-brigandine riding coat with moss/faded-blue wool and burgundy badge.
- Pants: reinforced wool riding hose/breeches with leather inner-leg panels.
- Boots: tall riding boots with practical dull-iron spurs.
- Gloves: supple rein gloves with reinforced palms and articulated cuffs.
- Belt: courier belt with dispatch case, reins loop, tack knife sheath, and utility pouch.
- Weapon: compact cavalry arming sword in a serviceable scabbard; no oversized lance silhouette at icon scale.
- Necklace: small route token/horsehead seal on leather cord, not a modern compass or medal.

## Officer Visual Inheritance

The portrait and gear inherit the Royal Stables' oak stalls, tack, worn leather, hay, daylight, courier supplies, and disciplined working-horse culture.
