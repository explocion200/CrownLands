# Defensive Commander Production Specification

## Portrait

- Function: veteran male fortress commander responsible for walls, gates, garrisons, and siege readiness.
- Reference environment: `assets/inner-castle/gatehouse.png`.
- Identity: powerful but human-scale Crownlands man in his early 50s, weathered dark complexion, shaved head, short gray beard, old brow scar, steady watchful expression.
- Clothing: heavier practical kit than the other officers: mail, charcoal brigandine, restrained plate at shoulders/arms/knees, heavy gloves, burgundy command cloth.
- Equipment: gate keys, practical heater shield, compact command mace or staff; no giant fantasy armor.
- Composition: full-body three-quarter view inside the Gatehouse beside oak gate mechanisms and wall tools, exterior daylight beyond the portcullis.
- Lighting: soft gate opening daylight balanced with dim natural interior light; no fiery spectacle.

## Common Gear Set

- Head: sturdy visor-up bascinet/sallet with mail protection and burgundy tie.
- Chest: dark brigandine and mail defensive cuirass with restrained iron plates.
- Pants: heavy wool hose with mail/plate knee and thigh reinforcement.
- Boots: stout wallwarden boots with hobnailed soles and stone-dust wear.
- Gloves: heavy repair/defense gauntlets suitable for chains, gates, and combat.
- Belt: wide key belt with gate keys, hammer loop, cord, and compact repair pouch.
- Weapon: practical Crownlands heater shield with worn burgundy field and simple gate-tower device; no shield-shaped building or glow.
- Necklace: small masonry/gate seal on a robust chain, shaped as a medieval authority token rather than a modern medal.

## Officer Visual Inheritance

The portrait and gear inherit the Gatehouse's fieldstone, oak gate, portcullis iron, chain mechanisms, wall tools, garrison readiness, and restrained Crownlands heraldry.
