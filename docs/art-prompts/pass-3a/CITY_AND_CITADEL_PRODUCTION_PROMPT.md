Generate one cohesive transparent-background production sheet containing six game map assets in a 3x2 grid, with flat chroma-key magenta background for matting and no text.

1. Top left: City Stage 1, frontier holding, source target `assets/castles/shack.png`.
2. Top middle: City Stage 2, fortified village, source target `assets/castles/fort.png`.
3. Top right: City Stage 3, keep settlement, source target `assets/castles/keep.png`.

5. Bottom middle: City Stage 5, major Crownlands city, source target `assets/castles/city.png`.

Shared camera and art direction:

- Elevated strategy-game view, 35-45 degree pitch, same viewing direction for all six assets.
- Soft natural daylight, light haze, realistic ambient shadow, no dramatic spotlight.
- Transparent final production; flat chroma-key magenta generation background only.
- Small integrated contact ground only, no rectangular terrain plate.
- Believable 14th-15th century frontier Europe, fictional Crownlands culture.
- Materials: fieldstone, dressed stone, timber framing, wattle and daub, limewashed plaster, oak beams, thatch, wood shingles, limited clay tile, packed earth, straw, iron hardware, cloth banners, rope, leather.
- Palette: parchment, warm earth, ochre, rust, timber brown, charcoal, moss, faded blue, burgundy, weathered gray, muted brass.
- Details: cracked plaster, rain staining, soot, repaired roofs, patched walls, muddy cart tracks, smoke, woodpiles, carts, barrels, stables, market/workshop cues.
- Negative: no glowing magic, no neon, no purple, no dragons, no impossible fantasy megacity, no pristine palace, no modern symbols, no text, no labels, no UI elements.

Progression requirements:

- Stage 1: modest timber hall/house, rough palisade, basic gate, shed, woodpile, fenced area, dirt road, barrels/supplies, one small banner.
- Stage 2: visibly evolved from Stage 1, more buildings, improved palisade, stronger gate, small watch tower, workshop, storage, early fieldstone construction, wider road wear.

- Stage 4: substantial stone curtain wall, gatehouse, multiple towers, denser internal buildings, workshops, stables, storage, smoke, road approach, more dressed stone and clay tile, irregular medieval growth.
- Stage 5: major regional city with impressive stone fortifications, substantial gatehouse, multiple towers, dense urban core, timber-framed buildings, civic/noble hall, market, workshops, stables, storehouses, smoke, carts, roads, banners.
- Crown Citadel: ultimate expression of the same architecture, layered defensive walls, major gatehouse, towers, internal royal keep/palace, courtyards, administrative buildings, barracks, storehouses, burgundy royal banners, restrained gilt, quality masonry.
