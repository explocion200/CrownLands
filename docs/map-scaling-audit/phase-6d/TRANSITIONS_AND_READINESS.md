# Phase 6D Transitions and Readiness

## Transition bands

The approved dominant-axis directional classifier is unchanged; exact diagonal ties continue to resolve North/South. Only North↔East and North↔West neighbors receive transition bands. Bands are deterministic, 96 px wide, and capped at 0.24 strength. They converge palette and edge treatment near the shared boundary without blending whole maps or changing regional identity. East↔South and South↔West remain unblended controls.

The study evaluated 1,925 cardinal neighbor pairs, including 103 cross-theme pairs and 52 banded target transitions. Complete Layer 1 (24 regions), complete Layer 2 (32 regions), and sequential 25- and 100-region runs retained aligned road sockets, reciprocal topology compatibility, and OPEN/GATED background independence.

## Readiness

Production-integration planning may begin after review. Planning may cover immutable package contracts, storage, validation receipts, controlled publication, and runtime loading, but must not activate regions. The next hard evidence gate should be a direct 10,000-map development run because Phase 6D proves the 1,000-map target but does not yet prove the conservative 10,000-map exposure target.

No additional foundations, edges, roads, or accents are currently recommended. Any future expansion must be justified by the direct 10,000-map result.
