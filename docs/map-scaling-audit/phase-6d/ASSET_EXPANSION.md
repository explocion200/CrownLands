# Phase 6D Asset Expansion

## Foundations

Two new 1448×1086 source plates were created for each locked theme. The build process harmonizes each source against its locked Phase 6B theme plate and retains low-frequency color and ground-texture variation. Foundation transforms are limited to none, horizontal flip, vertical flip, and 180-degree rotation so camera geometry remains valid. The study measured 48 base plate/transform presentations and 1,000 deterministic broad tone presentations.

## Perimeter edges

Sixteen full-side alternatives were added: North, East, South, and West segments for each theme. Segments are cut from approved directional artwork, touch the literal image boundary, remain narrow, and preserve one centered socket opening. Per-side base/alternate selection produced 64 perimeter combinations in the study; the most frequent occurred 25 times.

## Roads

Two full-map internal-road modules were added per theme. Every module terminates at the locked sockets N(724,0), E(1448,543), S(724,1086), and W(0,543). The edge approaches retain approved width and treatment while internal junctions, bends, and alignment vary. Together with the legacy route, the sample used nine road geometries and 12 theme-specific presentations. Usage was base 357, v2 301, and v3 342.

## Unchanged assets

No farmland, vegetation, rocks, hills, ponds, woodland, or other interior accent was added. The Phase 6B manifest hash is recorded by the Phase 6D manifest and validated before every checkpoint.
