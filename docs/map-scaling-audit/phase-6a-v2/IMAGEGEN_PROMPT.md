# Corrected Phase 6A ImageGen prompt

Mode: built-in ImageGen, new-image generation with five style references.

References: current Crownlands castle/city progression, Gold Camp, Training Stronghold, and Crown Citadel. They are style references only and are not baked into the terrain.

The prompt requested a clean 4:3 Crownlands player-region map with a grounded medieval, painterly-polished, slightly dimensional game-asset treatment matching the current structures. It required a predominantly open warm-earth/grass interior, restrained farmland, sparse hedges, two or three small woodland groups, isolated low rocks, and at least roughly 75 percent calm playable ground.

It required a narrow natural perimeter of thin tree line, low ridge, and scrub occupying roughly 6-9 percent of edge depth, exactly one centered North/East/South/West dirt-road opening, and no other edge exits. It explicitly prohibited buildings, cities, Camps, Strongholds, Citadel, gates, arrows, objectives, labels, UI, plazas, interaction pads, dark old-map styling, heavy forest, deep blocker masses, clutter, biome fragmentation, and multiple exits.
