# Directional ImageGen prompt set

Mode: four separate built-in ImageGen new-image calls.

Shared references: approved Phase 6A v2 for simplicity and density; current Crownlands Castle, Gold Camp, Training Stronghold, and Crown Citadel for camera, rendering, materials, lighting, scale, and shadow language.

Every prompt required a mostly open 4:3 player-region map, Phase 6A v2 decoration density, no baked runtime objects, a natural barrier beginning at and cropped by the literal image boundary, and exactly one centered North/East/South/West road passage. Every prompt prohibited the rejected deep-forest style, a floating ring, exterior ground margin, crowding, extra exits, city pads, labels, gates, arrows, buildings, and objectives.

Theme-specific additions:

- West: natural grassy plains, modest fields, restrained woodland, temperate greens.
- North: pale cold-season grass, cool earth, sparse winter vegetation, subtle patchy frost, no continuous snow.
- East: richer natural greens, sparse palm-like clusters, mossy rock, no rainforest or jungle takeover.
- South: dusty ochre earth, dry pale grass, sparse scrub, weathered rock, no dunes or fantasy wasteland.
