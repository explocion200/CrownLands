# Phase 6A art source of truth

Phase 6A treats the current approved Crownlands artwork as authoritative visual direction. The audit inspected all five city-progression assets, four Strongholds, Crown Citadel, Gold Camp, Warband Camp, Relic Camp, Deed Camp, and representative current regional maps before generating the approval slice.

The shared visual language is grounded medieval realism with polished painterly rendering, fixed high oblique/orthographic camera, dimensional stone and timber, warm weathered earth, muted olive vegetation, slate-gray stone and roofs, restrained heraldic red, soft northwest daylight, and soft grounded shadows.

The approval terrain must look like the space those unchanged runtime objects occupy. It must not restyle, redraw, or bake cities, Camps, Strongholds, Citadel, banners, or other runtime objects into the background.

Map detail remains restrained: broad calm gameplay ground, two to four major environmental ideas, readable roads, and selective fields/hedges. Empty terrain is intentional space for 40 cities, banners, numbers, and marches.
