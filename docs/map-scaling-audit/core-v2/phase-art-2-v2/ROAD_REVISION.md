# Road revision

Road topology, route sockets, and cardinal exits are unchanged. ART-2 v2 changes only the painted surface treatment.

The revised roads use an irregular progression from grass to worn grass, exposed soil, wheel ruts, hoof wear, and a subdued core track. Width varies locally, edges dissolve into terrain, and secondary stretches are partially reclaimed. Objective approaches strengthen through occupation wear rather than bright color or hard outlines.

The Crown Citadel preserves its special locked arrangement: four disconnected ceremonial approach segments stop before the center. The center contains diffuse occupation wear, not a joined road cross. Every other map retains its approved topology and exact sockets.

The dedicated before/after board demonstrates the reduction in apparent width and saturation at the same crop scale. Runtime QA confirms roads remain legible beneath city labels and routes.
