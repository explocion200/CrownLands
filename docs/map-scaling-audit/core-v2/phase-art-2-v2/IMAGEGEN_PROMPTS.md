# Image generation record

Mode: built-in ImageGen image-edit workflow. Each final 1448x1086 background was generated from its ART-2 candidate plus map-specific city/objective guidance. Generated outputs were copied into a separate ART-2 v2 development directory; ART-2 and prototype sources were not overwritten.

## Shared final prompt specification

Preserve the exact rectangular map, cardinal sockets, locked road geometry, climate identity, camera, lighting, city-safe negative space, and literal edge-touching perimeter. Repaint only environmental art into a higher-quality handcrafted Crownlands Core territory. Use painterly polished late-medieval materials, restrained natural daylight, compact shadows, purposeful terrain rhythm, and detail between protected city pockets. Roads must be narrower, softer, irregular wagon roads with ruts, hoof wear, exposed soil, grass-reclaimed edges, and objective approaches. Add natural occupation wear at structure sites with no circles, pads, plazas, markers, or baked runtime objects.

## Map-specific direction

- Citadel: prestigious maintained countryside, estate boundaries and productive fields; exact-center occupied ground; four disconnected approach-road ends; no Citadel painted.
- Ironwatch: defensible rocky terrain, hardened compacted center, supply wear, restrained ridges and earthwork traces; no Stronghold painted.
- Tower: contested warmer/drier strategic ground, crossing military tracks and modest earthworks; open near-center reservation; no Tower or pad.
- Deed: organic settled frontier, a small off-center subordinate hamlet, fences, plots, shed/cart and wagon lanes; no Camp painted.
- Support: the cleanest temperate map, subtle fields, hedges, drainage, orchard/woodland fragments and elevation; maximum clarity for 70 cities.

## Iteration record

Rejected outputs were not retained as candidates. The first Citadel attempt baked a Citadel and overused field patchwork. A later Citadel attempt joined the locked disconnected approaches. The first Ironwatch attempt baked the Stronghold. Final Citadel, Ironwatch, and Tower passes also received city-safe refinements to reduce visual weight near locked city pockets. Deed and West Support passed the first candidate review.
