# ART-2 v2 art results

## Crown Citadel

The center now reads as occupied royal land rather than empty grass. Four disconnected approach-road ends retain the locked topology and dissolve into compacted ceremonial wear around the exact-center Citadel footprint. Estate boundaries, productive fields, drainage, hedges, and small woodland groups add prestige without creating a plaza or competing with the runtime Citadel.

## Ironwatch

The exact-center Stronghold sits over harder, compacted military ground. Narrow feeder roads, wagon-turning wear, restrained ridges, old defensive traces, and stony approaches make the territory defensible without walling in the map. The latest refinement reduced the center treatment and moved visual weight out of city-safe pockets.

## Southwest Holding Tower

No Tower is painted. The exact approved reservation remains open over naturally worn strategic ground with crossing tracks and modest earthwork traces. The center is prepared for a future structure without becoming a visible objective pad.

## Deed Camp

The runtime Camp remains dominant. A small irregular cottage group, fences, tiny plots, shed/cart activity, and wagon lanes make the settled frontier credible while remaining subordinate to real cities. Roads and local ground wear merge into the camp position rather than stopping beneath a sprite.

## West Support

This remains the cleanest map for its 70-city capacity. Low-contrast field boundaries, grass variation, drainage, hedges, scattered stones, and small woodland groups provide authored terrain rhythm without reducing city readability.

## Visual assessment

All five candidates are materially richer and more intentional than ART-2 while retaining calm gameplay surfaces. The Citadel carries the highest visual prestige; Ironwatch reads through defensive composition; Deed tells a settlement story; the Tower site communicates strategic wear; and Support proves the approach scales to 70 cities without clutter.
