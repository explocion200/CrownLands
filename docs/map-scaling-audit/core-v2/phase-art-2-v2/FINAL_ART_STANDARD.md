# Final Core art standard

Core v2 ART-2 v2 is approved and authoritative for the permanent 25-map Crownlands Core.

## Locked visual rules

- Core maps are more handcrafted, detailed, and prestigious than outer generated player regions.
- Detail is purposeful and gameplay-readable, never randomly dense.
- Roads are thin, irregular medieval wagon roads blended into grass, worn soil, mud, gravel, wheel ruts, hoof wear, and reclaimed edges.
- Thick, bright, uniformly outlined strategy-game paths are prohibited.
- Citadel, Strongholds, Camps, and future Towers feel rooted through restrained packed earth, traffic wear, feeder paths, supply traces, and natural erosion.
- Artificial objective circles, pads, plazas, rings, and markers are prohibited.
- City-safe terrain pockets remain visually calm; authored detail flows between them.
- Natural perimeters touch the literal image boundary and remain narrow.
- Every Core map shares the approved camera, lighting, material language, terrain-detail scale, road language, and grounded painterly finish.
- Environmental storytelling responds to the objective identity without baking runtime objects into backgrounds.

## Objective placement

- Crown Citadel: exact center.
- Strongholds: exact center.
- Camps: near center; slight reviewed shifts permitted.
- Holding Towers: near center; slight reviewed shifts permitted.
- Future outer Fortresses: near center; slight reviewed shifts permitted.

## Geometry contract

City capacities, city IDs, city coordinates, objective reservations, road sockets, topology, blockers, transitions, adjacency, climate identities, map dimensions, and spawn rules remain authoritative and unchanged. Core spacing retains a 68 px hard minimum and 70 px preference. Outer generated player-region spacing remains 112 px.

## Next permitted art scope

ART-3 may rebuild only the five already-existing B1 backgrounds at `(-2,-2)`, `(-1,-2)`, `(-2,-1)`, `(-1,-1)`, and `(-1,0)`. It must reuse their approved geometry. Aurum Keep remains exactly centered; Camps and the Holding Tower retain their approved near-center reservations. ART-3 must not generate a new Core coordinate.
