| # | Board | Purpose |
| ---: | --- | --- |

| 3 | `03-west-climate.png` | Grassy temperate land, modest fields, mild woodland, muddy roads |
| 4 | `04-south-climate.png` | Dry frontier earth, scrub, rock, sparse productive traces |
| 5 | `05-east-climate.png` | Open lush terrain, humid earth, broadleaf edge vegetation, restrained drainage |

| 7 | `07-stronghold-territory.png` | Greybanner, Aurum, Swiftgate, and Ironwatch environmental cues with open centers |
| 8 | `08-camp-territory.png` | Edge mining, battle wear, grounded historic stone, and small settled-frontier cues |
| 9 | `09-tower-territory.png` | Contested open fields, broken earthworks, scars, and near-center Tower reservations |

## Review interpretation

- The boards show finish and composition language, not drop-in tiles.
- No board is a final map or a production asset.
- Panel dividers are presentation-only and must never appear in map art.
- Any isolated prop shown is an upper-bound example. The locked prop budget and city/objective clearance still control final composition.
- The Gold Camp panel intentionally concentrates mining at a study edge; a final Gold territory must keep those cues at the map perimeter.
- The Deed panel's cottage cluster is the maximum permitted settlement scale and must stay visually below runtime cities.
- Tower studies reserve open objective ground and do not define Tower architecture.
