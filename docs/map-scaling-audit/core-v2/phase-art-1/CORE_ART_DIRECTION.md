# Final Core Art Direction

## General terrain

Core terrain is a mostly open, handcrafted medieval landscape with broad readable ground, gentle tonal and material variation, irregular worn routes, and one primary territorial idea. Detail concentrates at the perimeter or in a small number of off-center clusters. Large empty gameplay areas are intentional.

Do not use a visible grid, repeated foundation stamp, mirrored quadrant, pasted rectangular patch, hard climate block, or regular chessboard distribution. Handcrafting may add regional character, but never illustration-scene density.

## Camera and scale

- Use the same elevated three-quarter isometric/orthographic family as the approved transparent structure art.
- Keep terrain features, walls, fences, carts, trees, and field rows at a scale that makes the 66–78 px runtime cities dominant.
- Preserve the 1448×1086 Core map canvas used by the approved prototypes.
- Preserve 68 px hard city spacing and prefer 70 px; never reduce capacity to create visual room.
- Keep the objective influence footprint open at exact or near center according to its locked placement rule.

## Lighting and shadow

- One shared soft daylight model across the 5×5 kingdom.
- Diffuse natural light with legible material planes and compact grounded shadows.
- Weather and climate may alter warmth, moisture, and sky softness, but not camera or light direction.
- No bloom, magical emissive color, deep cinematic darkness, or long cast shadows across city positions.

## Color and contrast

- Base palette: earth, moss, muted grass, weathered wood, cold/warm fieldstone, mud, parchment straw, and restrained mineral gray.
- Royal burgundy, faded indigo, brass, and gilt belong primarily to runtime structures and heraldry.
- Background saturation and local contrast remain lower than city/objective art.
- Macro color variation must feel natural and continuous; do not jitter color merely to hide repetition.

## Detail density

- Mostly open playable land.
- At most one major thematic cluster plus two medium clusters, or three medium clusters with no major cluster.
- The central 30% remains largely clear except for the authoritative objective.
- City placement zones receive prop, blocker, road, and shadow clearance.
- Scattered micro-props are not a substitute for meaningful territory identity.

## Perimeter

- The natural barrier begins at the literal image boundary with no padding, empty strip, fog frame, or outer terrain band.
- It remains narrow and does not invade the playable interior.
- Roads cut through deliberate openings at the edge.
- Climate changes rock/vegetation balance, not barrier thickness or world language.
- Outer silhouettes should be irregular and natural, never an artificial UI border.

## Roads

- Packed earth, mud, stone, ruts, wheel wear, puddling, and repaired crossings as climate permits.
- Routes bend and widen naturally near important objectives; they do not become broad blurred ribbons.
- Edge sockets and topology remain authoritative. Art adapts to geometry.
- Citadel approaches are better maintained; Swiftgate communicates movement; Ironwatch approaches feel defensible; ordinary roads remain practical.
- Roads are openings through the perimeter, not extra decoration, plazas, or city-sized junctions.

## Regional climates

| Region | Locked treatment | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North | Pale dormant grass, patchy frost, cold earth, gray rock, sparse winter trees, cool restrained color | Full snow blanket, tundra fantasy, ice glow, blizzard treatment |
| West | Healthy temperate grass, mild woodland, modest farmland, orchard/field traces, green countryside | Deep forest, oversaturated emerald, crowded agriculture |
| South | Dry grass, warm dusty soil, scrub, weathered dry rock, compacted roads | Endless sand, dunes, orange fantasy desert, total barrenness |
| East | Richer vegetation, slightly wetter ground, broadleaf accents, green-gray water/drainage where useful | Jungle walls, rainforest takeover, glowing plants, tropical-fantasy saturation |
| Center | Balanced temperate Crownlands with well-maintained mixed vegetation and stone | A fifth biome or abrupt neutral-color band |

## Transitions

The Core is one kingdom. Climate changes occur through narrow shared-edge material blending and gradual interior emphasis, not rectangular overlays. Center moves gently into west/east character; northwest cools toward North; southeast warms and becomes lusher. North↔West and North↔East require especially careful frost/vegetation convergence while keeping each territory legible.

## Baked-object prohibition

Final backgrounds contain no runtime city, Camp, Stronghold, Citadel, Holding Tower, Fortress, Gate, arrow, label, selection state, or UI. The ART-1 boards are reference studies only and are not eligible map backgrounds.
