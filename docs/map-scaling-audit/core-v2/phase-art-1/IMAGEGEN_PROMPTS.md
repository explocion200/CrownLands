# ART-1 Image Generation Briefs

The boards were generated as distinct raster studies with the approved Crownlands structures supplied as visual references. In every prompt, structure images were labeled as the authority for camera, material, finish, scale, light, and shadow. Approved Phase 6A directional samples, where used, were explicitly limited to simplicity and density guidance.

## Shared prompt lock

Each prompt required: polished painterly Crownlands environment art; elevated three-quarter orthographic view; grounded 14th–15th century materials; natural daylight; open gameplay space; restrained decoration; no runtime structures or UI; no text; no complete map; no magical glow; no tile repetition; and no watermark.

## Per-board prompt briefs

1. **General terrain** — four cropped studies of open earth/grass, a narrow barrier touching the frame, one muddy opening, and one restrained rural prop grouping. References: Castle, Citadel, Greybanner, Deed Camp, approved West density sample.
2. **North** — pale dormant grass, patchy frost, cold earth, gray rock, sparse winter trees, and a muddy winter opening; explicitly no full snow/tundra. References: Castle, Greybanner, Warband, Relic, approved North density sample.
3. **West** — temperate pasture, broken meadow, modest field/orchard edge, mild woodland/stone boundary, muddy road; explicitly no deep forest. References: Castle, Aurum, Gold Camp, Deed Camp, approved West density sample.
4. **South** — dry grass, dusty soil, scrub, weathered rock, sparse field traces, and one small earthwork/barricade cue; explicitly no dunes. References: Castle, Ironwatch, Warband, Deed, approved South density sample.
5. **East** — rich open grass, humid earth, restrained broadleaf vegetation, weathered rock, small drainage/water detail, muddy road; explicitly no jungle. References: Castle, Swiftgate, Relic, approved East density sample.
6. **Citadel territory** — four exact-center-ready influence studies with maintained routes, managed land, sparse civic stone markers, and restrained prosperity; Citadel itself forbidden. References: Citadel, Castle, Aurum, Phase A Citadel geometry only.
7. **Stronghold territory** — four open-center studies: training/drill trace, productive farmland, movement junction, and defensive earthwork; objectives forbidden. References: all four Strongholds and Castle.
8. **Camp territory** — corrected four-panel lock: perimeter-biased Gold mines, battle-worn Warband land, grounded Relic remains, and a small off-center Deed settlement; Camps forbidden. References: all four Camps and Castle.
9. **Tower territory** — open near-center reservations with worn field, broken earthworks, ditch/scar/burn traces, old barricades, and irregular patrol roads; Tower artwork and ring roads forbidden. References: Castle, Greybanner, Ironwatch, and both Tower prototype geometries only.

The first Camp and Tower drafts were rejected during internal review because they were too generic: Gold read as agriculture and Tower read as a neat ring-road motif. The corrected outputs above replace those drafts and match the locked territory rules.
