# Core Territory Profiles

Every territory gets one readable idea, expressed with the fewest cues that establish it. The objective remains the primary focal object.

## Crown Citadel

- Exact-centered Crown Citadel.
- Balanced central-kingdom palette, clean ceremonial road approach, slightly better-maintained countryside, managed land, and sparse noble/estate cues.
- Prestigious through order, maintenance, and material quality—not a plaza, cityscape, giant garden, or crown motif field.
- Keep the exact-center influence area clear for the 260 px runtime Citadel.

## Strongholds

All four are exact-centered and retain 60 city positions.

| Stronghold | Territory identity | Allowed cues | Forbidden interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Greybanner Hold | Military mustering and troop production | One drill-field trace/target group, military approach, sparse edge supply or wagon cue | Giant military base, permanent army camp, parade ground filling the map |
| Aurum Keep | Prosperous productive countryside | Good farmland, orchard, managed fields, storage/cart traces near edges | Literal gold land, treasure piles, urban market, palace estate |
| Swiftgate | Important travel corridor | Strong readable routes, natural crossroads, milestone/wayfinding cue, modest bridge or pass treatment | Extra exits, giant plaza, excessive roadside settlement |
| Ironwatch | Naturally defensive country | Stronger stone/ridge character, rocky approaches, one old wall/earthwork or barricade trace | Massive map-wide walls, ring fortress, clutter that competes with the Stronghold |

## Camps

Camps stay near center with slight adjustment permitted.

| Camp | Territory identity | Allowed cues | Forbidden interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warband | Lightly battle-worn troop territory | Churned mud, trampled ground, damaged fence, broken wagon/barricade, small supply trace, isolated old scar | Bodies, giant battlefield, siege tableau, excessive tents, heavy destruction |
| Deed | Settled frontier that awards cities | One restrained off-center cottage/shed/fence cluster, tiny cart/market trace, minor lanes, farm plots | A background town or city that competes with runtime cities |
| Relic | Old important place, grounded and historic | Old foundation, carved stone, weathered shrine, burial mound, broken column, small excavation, worn path | Magical glow, energy, giant temple, ruins biome |
| Gold | Mining/resource territory concentrated toward outer edges | One or two small mine entrances, rocky cuts, timber support, ore cart, spoil pile, worker track integrated with perimeter | Central quarry, literal gold terrain, huge mine complex, reduced 55-city interior |

## Holding Towers

- Near-centered reservation; no Tower artwork is invented or baked.
- Contested but not destroyed territory: worn open field, shallow defensive ditch, low old earthwork, isolated scar/burned vegetation, one old barricade remnant, sparse patrol/supply cue.
- Roads and earthworks use irregular broken arcs, never a complete arena or ring road.
- Keep 55 city positions and the near-center Tower footprint clear.

## Future outer Fortresses

- Near-centered reservation and the same limited-prop discipline.
- No Fortress art or actual Layer 1 map is produced in ART-1.
- Territory preparation must not alter published outer-region spacing, topology, or edge contracts.

## Support maps

Support maps are the cleanest Core maps and retain 70 city positions each. Identity comes from climate and geography rather than prop clusters:

- North Support: light winter, pale open land, minimal sparse vegetation.
- West Support: temperate grass/countryside, extremely restrained field or woodland trace.
- South Support: dry frontier ground, scrub, low rock, almost no thematic props.
- East Support: open lush ground and slightly wetter character without jungle density.

No Support map receives a pseudo-objective, landmark cluster, or background settlement.
