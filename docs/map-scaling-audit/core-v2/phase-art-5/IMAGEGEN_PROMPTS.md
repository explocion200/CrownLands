# ART-5 image generation record

The five backgrounds were created with the built-in image generation tool in image-edit mode. For each map, the deterministic ART-5 geometry draft was the edit target and approved ART-2 v2 / ART-3 / ART-4 backgrounds were style references.

Every prompt locked the 1448×1086 composition, four midpoint road sockets, internal road centerlines/topology, literal edge-touching perimeter, thin irregular medieval wagon-road treatment, calm city-safe pockets, and absence of baked runtime objects or UI.

## Per-map direction

- **East Deed:** muted lush/wet eastern countryside; one subordinate cottage/shed/fence/garden cluster; worn ground around `(742,533)` but no Camp or objective pad.
- **Swiftgate:** exact-center `(724,543)` kept clear; strongest traffic-wear identity in the Core; natural narrow crossroads and occupied ground without a plaza or Stronghold baked in.
- **East Support:** clean 70-city terrain; healthy grass, drainage, hedges and restrained agriculture; no objective or clutter.
- **SE Tower:** east-to-south climate blend; crossing military wear and restrained earthworks around `(736,555)`; no Tower, circle or foundation.
- **East Relic:** mossy reclaimed ruins, old paths and warmer southern ground; distinct from both approved Relic maps; no magic, Camp, temple or pad.

Negative constraints in all prompts excluded cities, objectives, Gates, arrows, labels, banners, troop text, selection UI, armies, magic, jungle overload, watermarks and text.
