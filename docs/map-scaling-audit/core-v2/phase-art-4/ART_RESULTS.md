| Map | Result |
| --- | --- |

All five candidates use thin irregular roads, literal-edge natural barriers, calm city pockets, compact grounded shadows, and the established Core material language. The north-to-east shift is gradual rather than a hard winter/tropical seam.

The visual-review gallery confirms that the five new maps belong beside the ten locked final-art candidates. ART-4 is approved for a development art checkpoint. Interactive Browser QA remains explicitly deferred—not passed—because the external Codex Browser permission layer denied the healthy local fixture. That deferred QA is not art-production-blocking, but it remains a mandatory production gate for the future consolidated 25-map Core review.
