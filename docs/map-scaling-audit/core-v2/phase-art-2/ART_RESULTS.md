# ART-2 results

All five candidates use the ART-1 structure-led language: elevated three-quarter terrain, compact grounded shadows, weathered earth/stone/timber materials, restrained color, narrow literal-edge barriers, and deliberate road openings. Runtime cities and objectives remain separate overlays.

## Crown Citadel

The healthy central grassland, small managed plots, orchard, and composed approach roads communicate a prosperous center without building a plaza or cityscape. The exact-center Citadel remains dominant. The terrain is in the same art family as the approved Crownlands structures: **yes**. It is materially more polished and coherent than the prototype: **yes**.

## Ironwatch

Rockier, subtly drier ground, restrained ridge fragments, and hardened approaches establish defense while leaving the exact-center Stronghold dominant. There is no map-wide fortress wall. Same structure art family: **yes**. Material improvement over prototype: **yes**.

## Southwest Holding Tower

Worn dry ground, limited earthworks, broken defensive traces, and open military approaches read as contested territory. No Tower is baked into the terrain and the approved reservation remains clean. Same structure art family: **yes**. Material improvement over prototype: **yes**.

## Deed Camp

A small off-center cottage/farm cluster sits in a verified clearance pocket and remains subordinate to runtime cities. The final correction removed its initial city-footprint contact. The approved near-center Camp remains a runtime overlay. Same structure art family: **yes**. Material improvement over prototype: **yes**.

## West Support

This is intentionally the cleanest candidate: open western grassland, very limited fields/orchard/woodland, and broad calm areas for 70 cities. Same structure art family: **yes**. Material improvement over prototype: **yes**.

## Shared Core reading

Camera, road rendering, daylight, border materials, and terrain detail scale are consistent across the set. Color and ground character progress gradually from maintained Center and temperate West toward the warmer/drier Southwest and South-Central maps. No hard biome seam was introduced.

The ART-1 prop rule passes on every candidate: no more than one major plus two medium clusters, or three medium clusters without a major. The generated-player-region 118-asset library was not modified.
