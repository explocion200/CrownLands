# ART-3 Results

## Warband Camp

The northern frontier is pale and cold without becoming an effects-driven fantasy biome. Trampled grass, cold exposed soil, wagon/hoof wear, damaged fence traces, and a restrained supply cluster establish military history. The runtime Warband Camp sits on compacted traffic ground and remains the visual centerpiece.

## Northern Relic Camp

The northern Relic map uses frost-worn remnants, exposed historic foundations, sparse cold vegetation, and old footpaths. It is materially distinct from the western Relic composition and contains no glow, magic, or oversized temple.

## Transitional Relic Camp

The western Relic territory uses greener pasture, field boundaries, hedges, weathered rural foundations, and a separate ruin arrangement. Its northern edge carries a restrained cold influence while the interior remains unmistakably western countryside.

## Northwest Holding Tower

The future Tower reservation is surrounded by crossing military tracks, worn strategic terrain, slight ditch/earthwork traces, and sparse defensive debris. There is no Tower art, circular foundation, platform, or baked objective marker.

## Aurum Keep

Aurum is exact-center and rooted into maintained wagon approaches, productive fields, orchards, pasture, and estate agriculture. Wealth reads through land use and supply activity; no literal gold decoration is present.

## Shared result

All five use the ART-2 v2 camera, daylight, painterly material scale, thin irregular roads, edge-touching natural barrier, and negative-space city pockets. The ten-map board confirms the ART-2 v2 and ART-3 maps belong to one Core family.
