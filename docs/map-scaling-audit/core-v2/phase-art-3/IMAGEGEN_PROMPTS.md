# ART-3 Image Generation Record

The five candidate backgrounds were created through the approved raster image-generation workflow, using each locked B1 prototype as the geometry/layout reference and the approved ART-2 v2 images plus `FINAL_ART_STANDARD.md` as the quality/style reference.

Every prompt locked these common constraints:

- exact 1448×1086 background with the existing four road sockets;
- no city, objective, Gate, Arrow, label, banner, troop, selection, route, or UI baked into the image;
- thin, irregular, muted medieval wagon roads with natural soil/grass blending;
- objective-aware packed earth and traffic wear without circles, pads, foundations, or plazas;
- calm negative-space pockets at the supplied city positions;
- a narrow climate-appropriate natural perimeter touching the literal edge;
- restrained ART-1 prop budget and no additional Core coordinate.

Map-specific prompting supplied the Warband, two distinct Relic, Tower-reservation, and productive Aurum environmental narratives defined in the phase brief. Two subsequent cleanup experiments were rejected: one erased too much Northern Relic identity and one introduced visible Aurum city-pad patterns. Those rejected variants were not copied into the deliverable set. Two accepted refinements reduced conflicts in the Transitional Relic and Northwest Tower candidates without changing locked geometry metadata.

Source generation copies remain in the image-generation output directory. The accepted, reviewable copies live under `benchmark-results/map/core-v2-phase-art-3/candidates/`.
