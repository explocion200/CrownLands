Shared-world standards

Game rules and fair play

These rules explain the expectations that keep Crownlands understandable, competitive, and safe for every ruler.

One player, one honest session

Use an account you are authorized to control. Do not impersonate another player, obtain access to another person’s account, share authentication credentials, or attempt to bypass the game’s active-session controls. Crownlands may invalidate sessions or actions that fail server authentication, integrity, or abuse checks.

Do not automate gameplay through bots, macros, scripted browsers, packet replay, modified clients, or other tools that perform actions without normal player input. Do not exploit race conditions, duplicate requests, stale interfaces, or network retries to obtain resources, duplicate rewards, or send orders that the interface would not normally permit.

Server results are authoritative

The shared server decides city ownership, troop availability, marches, combat, rewards, purchases, clan changes, and other multiplayer outcomes. Information on screen is a view of that state and may briefly lag during a reconnect. If a displayed estimate conflicts with a validated server result, the server result controls.

Do not attempt to write directly to protected game data, alter another player’s records, forge identity fields, or interfere with live functions. Report a reproducible issue instead of repeatedly testing it against other players.

Combat and expansion

Attacks, scouting, captures, protection periods, daily limits, and experience cooldowns are part of normal play. A city changing owners can reduce its level and redirect relevant troops according to the current rules. Players are responsible for checking targets, travel time, reports, and protection indicators before sending an order.

Weaker-kingdom protection and other safety rules are designed to limit extreme mismatches, not to guarantee that every holding is safe. Do not use alternate accounts, coordinated surrender, repeated throwaway captures, or other arrangements to manufacture experience, rankings, resources, or protected states.

Inactive kingdoms

A kingdom with no successful server login or heartbeat for 15 consecutive days automatically surrenders every holding except its protected main city. Troops owned by that ruler in surrendered holdings, marches, camps, and allied reinforcements are consolidated at the main city. Territory surrendered for inactivity is not restored.

After 20 consecutive inactive days, the remaining main city is released and that rulerâ€™s current-world slot is reset. The sign-in account and public identity remain available, but returning to the realm starts a fresh kingdom. Returning between days 15 and 20 cancels the pending world-slot reset.

Clans and communication

Clan leaders and officers may review applications, manage roles, and remove members according to the permissions shown in the interface. Allied reinforcements remain attributable to their contributor and may be returned or lost under the reinforcement rules.

Clan rallies are server-authoritative coordinated attacks against eligible objectives. Forming rally targets are visible only to current clan members, although assembly marches remain visible on the shared map. Committing troops removes the contributor’s active Peace Shield. The leader controls launch and cancellation, allies may withdraw only before launch, and only the leader may spend a Recall Horn on the launched combined army.

Clan names, descriptions, flags, and messages must not contain threats, harassment, hateful content, sexual content, personal information, scams, malicious links, or attempts to evade moderation. Competitive disagreement is allowed; abuse directed at a person is not.

Gold, items, and optional rewards

Gold, troops, items, cities, ranks, and other game resources have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for money. Purchase limits, cooldowns, inventory capacity, and eligibility checks apply even when a client displays an older state.

Optional rewarded advertising, when available, requires a deliberate choice and a completed reward signal from the advertising provider. Closing, blocking, skipping, or failing to load an advertisement does not grant a reward. Attempting to simulate completion events or replay a claimed intent is prohibited.

Responsible security research

If you discover a vulnerability, stop before accessing private data, changing another player’s state, degrading service, or extracting credentials. Preserve only the minimum details needed to explain the issue and report it through the public support channel. Do not publish active exploit instructions while the issue remains unresolved.

Updates and enforcement

Crownlands is actively developed. Balance values, limits, world layouts, protection rules, and feature behavior may change. Significant updates can require a restart, reset, migration, or refreshed client. Continuing to play after a published update means the current rules and server configuration apply.

Actions that threaten accounts, the shared world, or fair competition may be rejected, reversed where practical, rate-limited, or associated accounts may lose access. Enforcement decisions consider server records and the severity or repetition of the conduct.
