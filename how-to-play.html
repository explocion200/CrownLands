Player guide

From first city to island campaign

Learn the core Crownlands loop and the information to check before committing troops, gold, or time.

1

Begin a kingdom

Sign in with Google, choose The Crown Marches, and enter the shared realm. A new ruler claims an available starting city in a starter region. That city becomes the main city: the default rally point for certain returned troops, recovered losses, and rewards that need a stable destination.

The opening goal is not to attack every nearby marker. First identify the main city, its current troop count, the rate at which it produces gold and troops, and the neutral cities within practical marching distance.

2

Build production deliberately

Regular cities produce gold and troops in real time. Higher city levels create more victory points, which increase production and make the city more valuable. Upgrading costs gold, so each upgrade is a choice between stronger future income and keeping resources available for immediate actions.

Troops remain in the city that produced them until they march. That means a productive city can become a useful defensive reserve, but only if its troops are not repeatedly drained. Gold is held at the kingdom level and can be used across the realm.

Useful habits

  • Upgrade safer interior cities when the border is unstable.
  • Keep enough troops in exposed cities to discourage easy captures.
  • Use the owned-city list to compare levels, troops, and regions instead of searching every map manually.
  • Review production after gaining or losing cities because kingdom totals can change immediately.
3

Read the regional map

Crownlands is divided into connected island regions. Edge connections move the view between neighboring regions, and armies follow valid routes across those connections. Cities, camps, Strongholds, and the Crown Citadel appear as interactive markers over the map.

Select an owned city to review its troops, level, production, and available actions. Select a neutral or rival city to see scouting, attack, and information options. The home indicator recenters the view when the main city is outside the current screen.

4

Scout before attacking

A scout is a small march that reveals a snapshot of a target after arrival. The report can show troops, city level, wall contribution, total defense, and relevant bonuses. Intelligence is time-sensitive: a player may reinforce or move troops after a scout arrives.

Use the combat preview together with the latest report. The preview explains estimated attack and defense power, likely losses, survivors, and the expected capture or defeat result. Estimates are planning tools rather than a reason to ignore travel time or changing ownership.

Do not send based only on the number printed over a city. Walls, skills, Stronghold effects, allied reinforcements, and recent troop movement can change the actual defense.
5

Send armies and protect holdings

Choose a source city, target, and troop amount to create a march. The server validates ownership, available troops, route, protection rules, and other requirements before accepting the order. When the march arrives, the server resolves the outcome and records a report.

Successful captures transfer the city and reduce its level by one, never below Level 1. Failed attacks can still award reduced experience. A captured city may have a temporary experience cooldown, which lowers repeated capture rewards without preventing future attacks.

Defense is preparation

City defenders benefit from stationed troop strength and city walls. Upgrades and defensive skills improve the cost of taking an important holding. If an attack is already marching, decide whether to reinforce the target, move vulnerable troops, or accept the battle and preserve resources elsewhere.

6

Contest camps, Strongholds, and the Citadel

Resource camps reward a ruler who defeats their defenders and holds the position for the required time. Gold and troop camps provide production-based rewards, while Deed and Relic camps use their own award rules. Camps are temporary objectives rather than ordinary permanent cities.

Strongholds grant strategic benefits to their controller. Different Strongholds can improve gold, training, defense, or movement. The Crown Citadel is the realm’s central prestige objective: its current controller receives the Citadel effect, and the Reign Ledger records cumulative control time.

7

Develop skills and use items with purpose

Hero levels award skill points. The current skill paths improve attack power, city walls, gold production, troop production, upgrade efficiency, march speed, or the return of battle losses. Skill choices apply across the kingdom, so invest according to the type of campaign you are actually playing.

Shop and reward items provide temporary protection, production, concealment, faster transfers, or army recall. Most items are bought with in-game gold and have daily limits. Read the description and duration before use; a timed effect is most valuable when it supports a planned sequence of actions.

8

Coordinate through a clan

Clans provide a shared identity, membership roles, chat, applications, and allied map information. Clan members cannot treat allied cities as ordinary enemy targets. Instead, they can send reinforcements to eligible allied holdings.

Reinforcement troops continue to belong to their sender. The sender may recall them, the holding owner may send them home, and battle losses are applied when the position is attacked. Good clan defense depends on clear timing and knowing who is responsible for each contribution.

First-session checklist

  • Locate the main city and review its production.
  • Identify two or three nearby neutral cities rather than spreading in every direction.
  • Upgrade where the resulting production will matter most.
  • Scout uncertain targets and read the resulting report.
  • Keep a defensive reserve instead of marching every troop.
  • Open the reports panel after each arrival to understand the result.
  • Review skills only after deciding whether the kingdom needs economy, defense, or mobility first.
