See what every city and soldier actually changes

Crownlands has one connected system: city levels create value and production, skills specialize that value, and battles spend attack power against one wall and then one garrison. Change the examples below to see the same calculations used by the game.

Forecasts are snapshots. A live defender can produce troops, receive reinforcements, change bonuses, or finish a wall repair before your army arrives.

Show the city math

Victory points: floor(6 + 4L + 2L1.35).

Troops/hour: victory points × 10, then Royal Granaries and the applicable objective package add percentages of that base.

Gold/hour: floor(20 × 1.115L−1) × 15 through Level 100. Above 100, the Level 100 value grows 8% per additional level.

Owner garrison: floor(stationed troops × 1.30 × (1 + Shieldwall Discipline% + personal objective% + shared clan objective%)). City level is never part of soldier defense.

Wall: floor(200 + 28,858 × (city level − 1)), then Stoneworks alone strengthens that one wall. Objective support never changes wall power.

Repair added by a hit: full-breach repair window × wall damage ÷ full wall power.

Invest in safer cities

Upgrading raises future income and defense, but concentrated levels also concentrate value. A border city can become a rich target before its troops and wall are ready.

Stored-production rewards scale

Many rewards are expressed as hours of production. Daily Mission targets and payouts are calculated from a server capacity snapshot at generation and stay locked until the next 00:00 UTC reset.

Gold and troops solve different problems

Gold accelerates development and buys actions. Troops defend holdings and become attack power only when committed to a march.

Show the battle math

Attack power: floor(committed troops × 1.25 × (1 + Swordmastery%)). Maximum Swordmastery raises this to 2 power per troop.

Soldier defense: floor(troops × 1.30 × (1 + Shieldwall Discipline% + personal objective% + shared clan objective%)). Maximum Shieldwall without objective support is 2.08 power per soldier.

Wall phase: the current wall absorbs attack power first. Penetrating power is max(0, attack power − current wall).

Garrison phase: penetrating power must be strictly greater than the combined owner and reinforcement defense. Equality is a defensive hold.

Winning survivors: troops represented by attack power remaining after 100% of wall and garrison defense is resolved.

Wall hold losses: defenders lose at most 10%. After a breach where the garrison still holds, defender losses scale with pressure and stop at 82%.

Every handoff preserves it

Combat captures, relinquishment, inactivity, neutralization, and neutral claims carry the integrity and absolute deadline.

Upgrades do not recalculate it

Level or ownership changes during an active repair do not lengthen or shorten that deadline.

A breached wall stays at zero

Further attacks against an already breached wall do not restart its timer.

Protected raids do not persist damage

The raid may remove defenders, but its wall damage is not written to the city.

Important exceptions

The server still checks the target and both kingdoms

Regular city

Uses the two-stage wall and garrison siege. Main cities cannot be attacked. A successful capture reduces the city by one level but carries any active wall deadline forward.

Stronghold and Citadel

Use the same single-wall siege. Strongholds use effective Level 50 fortification timing and the Citadel uses Level 100. Weaker-kingdom attack protection does not apply to these objectives.

Reward camp

Every neutral camp starts with 20,000 troops. Camps have no level, wall, Stoneworks, Shieldwall, or objective-defense bonus: each stationed troop and reinforcement contributes exactly 1.00 defense power. Camps cannot be shielded, do not count as cities, and may require a hold timer before rewards settle.

Clan reinforcement

Each contributor adds troops with that contributor’s applicable objective-defense package. Reinforcements never copy the city’s level defense, wall, or Stoneworks.

Clan rally

Each participant keeps snapshotted attack strength. The combined force attacks under the rally lifecycle and still passes every ownership, protection, route, and anti-farming check.

Weaker-kingdom protection

At 2× King Power, attacks become capped protected assaults: first breach, then a possible capture assault. At 2.5× and above, they become capped, non-capturing raids with defender losses limited to 10% and no persistent wall damage.

Scouting

A successful scout is an exact ten-minute snapshot. Attack strength is locked at launch, but defense remains live until arrival, so a forecast is evidence rather than a guarantee.

Directional farming prevention

Normal fighting remains available, but repeated low-city handoffs of freshly claimed neutral cities can create a directional restriction between the involved players.

Use the guide as a decision checklist

  1. Scout the target and check how old the report is.
  2. Compare your launch-locked attack power with the current wall and garrison separately.
  3. Check protection rules, reinforcements, travel time, and the wall-repair deadline.
  4. Send enough to cross the capture threshold with room for live defensive changes.
