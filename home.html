Core gameplay pillars

A kingdom is built through connected decisions

Crownlands rewards planning across economy, information, territory, and allies—not one isolated upgrade.

01

Build cities

Upgrade holdings to strengthen long-term production and kingdom value. Every city is also a place troops must be positioned and defended.

02

Produce resources

Gold funds development while troops accumulate in the city that trained them. Growth matters most when it supports a clear territorial plan.

03

Scout rivals

Fresh intelligence separates walls, garrisons, reinforcements, and bonuses. Reports expire, so timing a decision matters as much as reading it.

04

March armies

Choose a source, destination, and force while the server validates the route and arrival. Armies remain committed while the realm continues changing.

05

Capture territory

Break a wall, defeat the defending soldiers, and take cities that extend your reach. A damaged frontier can invite a counterattack from another ruler.

06

Join clans

Reinforce allied holdings, share map context, and combine forces through rallies. Officers and leaders can form rallies; members can contribute troops.

07

Contest objectives

Reward camps offer temporary opportunities, Strongholds grant specialized advantages, and the Crown Citadel anchors realm-wide prestige.

08

Develop a ruler

Earn levels, specialize skills, pursue achievements, and begin assembling gear. These systems support strategy without replacing cities and armies.

What Crownlands is

A strategy game about information, timing, and territory

Crownlands begins with one Main City, but its real strategy comes from the network around it. Regular cities produce troops and gold over time. Border holdings create risk. Strongholds provide specialized advantages. The Crown Citadel records the rulers who can take the center of the realm and hold it.

There is no fixed campaign path. A ruler may invest in production, specialize through hero skills, pressure a damaged wall, reinforce a clanmate, or wait for better intelligence. The public guides on this site explain the same formulas, timings, and server rules used by the live game so that a new player can make an informed decision before signing in.

01

Build a realm, not a single base

Claim connected cities, compare production, and decide which holdings should grow, defend the frontier, or stage the next march. Troops remain where they are produced until ordered elsewhere, so geography always matters.

02

Turn battle results into better decisions

Reports separate the wall from the garrison and show attack, defense, objective support, skills, reinforcements, survivors, and losses. The point is not only to announce who won - it is to make the result auditable.

03

Fight for objectives larger than one city

Reward camps create short opportunities. Strongholds change training, movement, gold, or soldier defense. The Citadel is the realm's prestige objective, with scheduled Legion pressure and a cumulative Reign Ledger.
