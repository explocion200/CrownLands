How the realm works

A world larger than one screen

The current Crownlands world contains 20 connected regions. A ruler may begin in a starter region, capture neutral cities, improve production, and then use valid regional routes to reach new territory. Owned cities remain part of the same kingdom even when they sit on different maps. Marches, reports, clan support, production, and King Power continue to reflect the whole realm.

Regions are not interchangeable containers. Their city placement, routes, objective positions, visual terrain, and available space create different strategic pressures. A border city can become a staging point for a cross-region march, while a safer interior holding may be better suited to long production upgrades. The world selector helps rulers understand where a city sits before they commit troops or gold.

Starter and midgame regions

The connected world also includes fifteen handcrafted maps that create more room for new kingdoms and regional progression. Starter regions provide places where a ruler can establish the first network of cities. Midgame regions provide additional routes and holdings as that kingdom grows. These labels describe their role in the current world layout; they do not lock a player into a scripted campaign.

Active balancing

Capacity belongs to each region

Each map has its own city capacity and layout balance. Those values can differ because a region’s routes, objective space, city spacing, and role are different. Capacity is not one broken global number: it is a per-region balance setting that may change as testing shows how much room each map needs.

When a region approaches its intended population, new rulers may begin elsewhere or a future expansion route may provide more space. Existing ownership and movement rules remain authoritative while those world-growth systems are developed.

In development

Dynamic realm expansion

Crownlands currently has 20 connected regions. Development work is preparing the world system so additional maps can spawn or be added when population and gameplay demand require more space. The exact shape, timing, and balance of future regions will continue to depend on testing.
