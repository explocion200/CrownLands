A community for a game in active development

Feedback is most valuable when it is tied to the live Crownlands experience. A screenshot of a hard-to-read report, the time an army appeared delayed, the region where a route felt unclear, or the reason a daily objective felt out of scale can all lead to a concrete improvement. General reactions are welcome too, but specific context makes a report easier to reproduce and evaluate.

Not every suggestion becomes a rule. Crownlands has to preserve fair multiplayer outcomes, server authority, readable mobile controls, performance on crowded maps, and an economy that works for new and established kingdoms. Development notes and the public roadmap explain the direction without promising exact dates.

✦

Share gameplay feedback

Describe the action you were taking, what information was visible, what you expected, and what made the choice confusing or satisfying. Feedback about reports, profiles, flags, settings, map readability, and mobile use is especially useful during the current polish work.

⚒

Report a reproducible bug

Include the approximate time, browser and device, region or city involved, visible error text, and the last action before the issue. Search the tracker first and keep one report focused on one problem. Never include passwords, auth codes, tokens, or private account data.

⚖

Suggest a balance change

Explain which type of kingdom is affected and provide an example. A useful balance suggestion separates city growth, troop production, travel time, walls, soldier defense, objective bonuses, and limited items instead of treating every power source as the same number.

⚑

Find clan conversation

Clans are live in the game, with roles, gifts, weekly goals, reinforcements, and rallies. Discord can help rulers compare strategy or look for a group, but clan membership and all authoritative actions still happen inside Crownlands.

What makes a report useful?

  1. Name the screen or system. For example: scouting report, clan rally, city upgrade, daily reward, or regional movement.
  2. Describe the starting state. Include the region, city role, browser/device, and whether the game had just reconnected.
  3. List the action and result. Say what you tapped or clicked, what appeared, and what you expected instead.
  4. Add safe evidence. A cropped screenshot and approximate time help. Remove email addresses, account identifiers, private chat, and tokens.
  5. Separate defects from suggestions. A bug means the implemented rule failed; a balance or UI suggestion proposes a different rule or presentation.

Respectful conduct

Strategy disagreement is welcome; harassment, hate, threats, scams, private-data disclosure, exploit abuse, and attempts to manipulate rankings are not. Community spaces supplement the formal Terms and Game Rules.

Privacy first
