System handbook

The Crownlands systems at a glance

Use these short references to find the deeper guide for the decision in front of you.

Cities and levels

Cities generate value, gold, and troops. Upgrades improve future output but also concentrate more value in a holding that must be defended.

Read city growth basics

Troops and marches

Troops stay in their source city until ordered elsewhere. Routes, travel time, available forces, protection, and ownership are validated when a march is created.

Learn army movement

Combat and walls

An attack must first break one physical wall before its remaining power fights the garrison. Reports separate both stages and explain losses.

Read battle reports

Scouting and reports

Successful scouting provides time-limited intelligence. Treat it as a dated snapshot because soldiers, walls, reinforcements, and ownership can change.

Read the scouting guide

Reward camps

Camps are temporary objectives with their own defenders and holding rules. Their production, deed, or relic rewards differ from ordinary city ownership.

Compare objective types

Strongholds and Citadel

Strongholds grant specialized strategic effects. The Crown Citadel is the central prestige objective and records cumulative control in the Reign Ledger.

Study the Citadel

Clans and rallies

Clan members can reinforce eligible holdings. Leaders and officers can form rallies while members contribute troops through the War Room.

Read clan coordination

Items and Bag

Items support planned protection, production, transfer, concealment, or recall actions. Gear is in its beginning stages and remains a developing progression layer.

See the gear foundation

Skills and presets

Attack, defense, and utility skills specialize a ruler. Presets store exact allocations and help established kingdoms switch deliberately.

Plan a skill build

Achievements

Achievements are live and being polished. They provide longer progression goals while Daily Quests remain a separate UTC-based activity loop.

Read the progression note

Leaderboards and King Power

Top 100 rulers and Top Clans are separate views. King Power summarizes kingdom strength for comparison without granting clan roles or gameplay permissions.

Understand kingdom growth

Install as a web app

Supported browsers can install Crownlands for quick launching. Installation keeps the same account and live web build rather than creating a separate game.

Get installation help
